## Verdict (written 2026-10-15 from the tables below; re-running refreshes the tables, not this text)

**The matchup tables do not find a WR/TE edge. The one real signal, a player's own recent usage, is already in the WR/TE lines. Two RB signals are left to watch; neither is bettable yet.**

1. **WR/TE matchup mismatches (alignment x what the defense allows from each alignment, man/zone, 1-high/2-high, personnel, depth of target): no information.** Adding the defense or mismatch features to a player's own profile does not raise out-of-sample AUC for targets, receptions, yards, route share, yards per route or targets per route (increments -0.017 to +0.012; 1 of 36 intervals clears zero, and that one is XGBoost-only on targets per route), including when only a short list fixed in advance is used. 402 best-vs-worst-matchup decile tests land where chance puts them (6% at p < 0.05, best Benjamini-Hochberg q 0.67). A true gap of about 8 points would have shown; the largest seen was 9.5 (a deep-use receiver against a defense that gives up depth, on targets per route), which is what the best of 400 tries looks like.
2. **His own usage trend is real, and for WR/TE the books already price it.** Out of sample the compact usage model scores AUC 0.62 on targets, 0.63 on route share and 0.58 on receiving yards (top-score decile beats his normal 67-69% of the time on targets and route share vs 27-28% in the bottom decile) and cuts raw-stat error 2-3% in every season. On the 2026 lines (weeks 4-5, about 250 each for rec yds and receptions) the signal replicates (+0.12 and +0.20 SD of the stat per SD of score vs our projection; history +0.16 and +0.19) but the book moved its line by +0.16 and +0.23, leaving -0.02 and -0.03 (interval about +/-0.14): nothing left to bet.
3. **RBs:** (a) *own usage trend*: strong and stable (+0.19 to +0.31 SD per SD in every season 2022-25, AUC 0.61-0.63) and the book does not move its line with it, but 2026 weeks 1-5 (260 lines) show only +0.08 vs our projection (interval -0.06 to +0.22), so it is neither confirmed nor refuted: the one thing worth a forward test. (b) *soft run defense -> more rush yards*: +2.5 to +3.8 yards per SD in 2022-24 (also from nflverse alone), +0.5 in 2025, about zero in 2026 weeks 1-5, and the book already moves its line ~60% of the old effect: it looks gone, do not bet it. (c) *defense zone rate -> RB receptions*: real but tiny (+0.08 receptions per SD).
4. **The one place defense features clearly predict is RB efficiency** (yards per carry above his trailing average: AUC 0.51 -> 0.54, best vs worst run-defense decile 60% vs 43%, BH q 0.04), but it fades in 2025-26 and yards per carry is not a market.

**What this means for betting:** no change to the model or the app. A binary "will he beat his normal" call is possible (AUC 0.58-0.63 on usage), but it is only worth money where the line has not already absorbed it, and so far that is untested territory for RBs only. Forward-test H1 on weeks 6+ (registry at the bottom), and a clean 2025 WR/TE re-pull would add a fourth season of power.
